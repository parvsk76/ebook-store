About Parveen Kumar

Parveen Kumar is a food technologist and R&D professional with over twelve years of hands-on experience in bakery systems, non-dairy and chocolate systems, and enzyme technology. He holds an M.Tech in Food Technology and a PGDM in Operations Management, and has worked across bakery, food manufacturing and ingredient companies in India.

Drawing on that industry experience, Parveen writes practical e-books that translate food science into clear, usable guidance: from sourdough, croissants and commercial cake premix formulation to everyday home-kitchen recipes. His catalog also reaches beyond the kitchen, with guides on stock-market basics, communication and confidence, wellness, workplace AI, political science and fiction.

🧪Science-first approach: formulation logic and troubleshooting explained simply.
🍞Industry-tested: lessons from lab, pilot and production-scale bakery work.
🌏Global & desi: recipes and guides for home cooks and professionals alike.
📘Official publications: every title links directly to Google Play Books.
